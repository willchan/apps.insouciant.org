Privacy policy

pandamini backup · last updated 6 November 2026

pandamini backup is used only by its owner, on their own Google account.

  • What it accesses: only the Google Drive files and folders it creates itself (the drive.file permission). It cannot see any other files in the Drive.
  • What it stores there: encrypted backups of the owner's own server. They are encrypted before upload with a password that Google never receives.
  • What it collects: nothing. It has no website accounts, analytics or tracking, and it sends no data to anyone other than Google Drive.
  • Sharing: no data is shared with, sold to or used by anyone else, and Google user data is not used to train AI models.
  • Removing access: the owner can revoke it at any time in their Google Account under Security, Third-party connections.
